ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology with care.

MediCare AI is a student-friendly healthcare information and intake prototype.

✚

Making health conversations easier.

The goal is to help people organize symptoms, health history, allergies and basic measurements before speaking with a healthcare professional.

What this prototype can do

  • Collect symptom descriptions through a guided chat
  • Save optional health profile details locally
  • Display a simple health information summary
  • Offer general educational safety guidance
